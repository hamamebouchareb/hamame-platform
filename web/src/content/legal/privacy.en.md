## Privacy Policy

### 1. Who we are

Hamame is an exam-preparation platform for Algerian students of medicine, dentistry and pharmacy. This policy explains which personal data we process, why, and what your rights are, in line with Law No. 18-07 of 10 June 2018 on the protection of individuals in the processing of personal data.

### 2. Data we collect

- Account: email address and/or phone number, full name, password (stored only in an irreversibly hashed form), faculty, year, university and wilaya as you provide them, a link to a profile photo if you add one, and your language and theme.
- Sign-in with Google (optional): a stable identifier provided by Google, linked to your account.
- Study activity: sessions, answers, scores, notes, flashcards, day streaks, progress, badges, friends and leaderboards.
- Subscription: plan, dates, amount, payment method, payment reference or the activation code used. Payments are made outside the platform and we do not store any bank card number.
- Notifications: in-app notifications and, if you enable them, your browser push subscription.
- Technical data: IP address and logs kept by our hosting providers, used for security and abuse prevention.

### 3. Why we use it

- To provide the service you asked for: creating your account, offering questions and courses, tracking your progress.
- To manage your subscription and activation codes.
- To send service messages: email or phone verification, password reset, notifications.
- To protect the service against abuse and unauthorized access.
- To produce aggregated statistics, for example how answers to a question are distributed.

We process your data with your consent, given at sign-up, and to perform the service you request.

### 4. Artificial intelligence

Some features (hints, explanations) send to an AI provider (Anthropic) the question text, its options, its place in the curriculum and an approximate performance level on the module. We do not send your name, email or identifier. AI answers can be wrong: please double-check them.

### 5. Providers and hosting

We use: Supabase (database, Ireland region), Railway (application server), Vercel (website), Resend (email delivery), Twilio (SMS delivery), Google (optional sign-in) and Anthropic (AI features). We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising. Some of these providers are located outside Algeria, so your data may be processed abroad. ### 6. Browser storage

Today we use no advertising cookies and no analytics tools. The site stores in your browser's local storage: your sign-in token, your language, your theme, your choice to skip the welcome screen and your current study session.

### 7. Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. If you delete your account, your personal identifiers (email, phone, name, photo) are erased or replaced, and your study activity is detached from your identity and kept in anonymous form for statistics. Payment records are kept for [LEGAL PERIOD TO CONFIRM] to meet accounting obligations.

### 8. Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, ask for its deletion, object to certain processing, withdraw your consent and obtain a copy of your data. You can export your data and delete your account from your account, or write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. You may also lodge a complaint with the national authority for personal data protection (ANPDP).

### 9. Security, age, changes

We protect your data with password hashing, secure connections and restricted access, but no security is absolute. The service is intended for people aged 18 or over; under 18, a legal guardian's consent is required. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version and we will tell you about important changes.
